How it Works

From idea to live page
in three steps

No build tools, no deployment pipelines — everything happens in the browser.

1

Drag Components

Pick from 75+ components in the palette. Drag onto the canvas. Each component has sensible defaults so you can see results instantly.

2

Configure Properties

Customize styles, bind data, add interactions — all in the right panel. Changes reflect immediately on the canvas.

3

Publish

One click to deploy. Your page runs on our SSR engine — fast, SEO-optimized, and always online.
